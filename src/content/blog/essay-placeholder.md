## The app I never finished

For a long time I had one big project in my head: a single app that would track my schedule, my habits, my
grades and my sleep. I started it four times. I never got past the settings page.

What I did finish, over and over, were small things. A script that pulls the morning announcements. A bot that
pings me before a deadline. Each one took a weekend, and each one I still use.

## Why small wins

A small tool has one job, so it is obvious when it works. There is no roadmap to fall behind on. When it breaks,
there are only a few places the bug can hide.

> Finishing a small thing teaches you more than starting a big one.

The big app is still a good idea. But now I build it the same way: one small piece at a time, each one useful on
its own.
